Before you start

  1. Open the form while you have internet access, before you travel. The browser then keeps a copy of it, so it still opens and saves submissions without a connection. They upload the next time you are online.
  2. Read the approved information sheet and get written consent first. If consent is not given, stop and close the form.
  3. Never enter names, phone numbers or other details that identify a respondent.
  4. Ask every question marked [CORE]. Ask the others as time allows.
  5. Record audio only where the participant has agreed to it, and write your observations in the final section.
